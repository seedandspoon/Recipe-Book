# Almanac

A year of your life, with the people you love.

Almanac holds the things you'd love to do with the people you care about (a weekend with Mum, walnuts at Dad's in autumn, a gift you spotted, a tradition you'd like to start) and brings each one back when the time is right: in its season, a few weeks before a birthday, or when you look at the months ahead.

It is one HTML file, like the Recipe Book next door. No account, no server.

## On your phone

1. Open the page in Safari (on Android, Chrome).
2. Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. It opens full screen from its own icon and works offline after the first visit.

## How it thinks

- **Now** shows what is timely today: dates coming up in the next six weeks (eight for Christmas) with the ideas waiting for them, what is in season, what is worth planning before its season starts, and one or two someday ideas each week.
- **Year** draws the year as a wheel (seasons round the rim, birthdays and occasions as dots, seasonal ideas as arcs, moments lived as ticks) and lists the twelve months ahead. *Looking back* shows what you've lived.
- **People** keeps their dates, what you know about them, ideas for them and the moments you've shared.
- **Ideas** is everything, searchable: traditions, seasonal, occasions, someday, planned, lived, let go.

When you type an idea, Almanac reads it: names of your people, seasons and months, Christmas or a birthday, "every year", and whether it sounds like a gift, a trip or something to make. Every guess shows as a chip you can change.

How early things come back: gifts four weeks ahead (six for Christmas), trips and weekends eight weeks ahead, everything else three.

## Reminders outside the app

A web page can't send notifications on its own, so Settings can create a calendar file instead: birthdays two weeks ahead, holidays three weeks ahead, the turn of each season, and a monthly "look at the months ahead" on the first Sunday. Import it once; it repeats every year by itself.

## Your data

Everything stays in the browser on your phone. Settings → **Save a backup** from time to time (or **Copy it** into a note), and **Restore a backup** to move to a new phone.

Open the page with `?today=2026-12-01` to see what it will look like on another day.
